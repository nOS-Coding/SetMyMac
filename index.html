How It Works

Your Mac, set up in 5 steps

  1. 1 Choose a profile — Writer, Developer, Gamer, Composer, or one of five others.
  2. 2 Step through the selection screens. Pick browsers, IDEs, emulators, DAWs, and more.
  3. 3 Review your selections and hit Install.
  4. 4 The app downloads and installs everything, reporting progress in real time.
  5. 5 Close the app and start using your machine.
